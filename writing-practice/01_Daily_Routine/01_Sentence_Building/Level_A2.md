## 💡 Vocabulary Hints
`always` · `usually` · `often` · `sometimes` · `never` · `take a shower` · `get dressed` · `drink coffee` · `finish work` · `cook dinner` · `do the dishes` · `before` · `after`

## 🧱 Sentence Frames
```
I + [adverb] + [verb] + ...            → I usually take a shower in the morning.
He/She + [adverb] + [verb-s] + ...     → She often drinks coffee at 9.
I + [verb] + ... + before/after + I + [verb] + ...
                                      → I brush my teeth after I have breakfast.
```

**Ví dụ:** `I get up early. (usually)` → **I usually get up early.**
