## 💡 Vocabulary Hints
`get up` · `have breakfast` · `bread` · `rice` · `noodles` · `coffee` · `tea` · `by bus` · `by motorbike` · `on foot` · `at home` · `at the office`

## 🧱 Sentence Frames
```
Q: What time do you ...?     → A: I ... at [time].
Q: What do you have for ...? → A: I have [food] for [meal].
Q: How do you go to ...?     → A: I go to ... by [vehicle] / on foot.
Q: Where do you ...?         → A: I ... at/in [place].
```

**Lưu ý:** ❌ "At 6." → ✅ **"I get up at 6 o'clock."** (luôn trả lời câu đầy đủ)
