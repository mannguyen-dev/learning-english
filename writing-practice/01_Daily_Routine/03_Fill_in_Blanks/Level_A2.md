## 💡 Grammar Hints
| Quy tắc | Ví dụ |
|---|---|
| He/She/It + V-s | get → gets, drink → drinks |
| Tận cùng -ch, -sh, -s, -x, -o → thêm -es | watch → watches, go → goes, wash → washes |
| Phụ âm + y → bỏ y + ies | study → studies |
| have → has | She has lunch. |
| at + giờ / at night · in + buổi · on + thứ | at 7, in the morning, on Sunday |

## 🧱 Sentence Frames
```
She + V-s + ... + at/in/on + [time].   → She gets up at 6 in the morning.
```
