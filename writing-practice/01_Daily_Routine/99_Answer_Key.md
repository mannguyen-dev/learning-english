## 01 · Sentence Building · A1, Part A
1. I have breakfast at 7 o'clock.
2. I brush my teeth in the morning.
3. I go to work at 8 o'clock.
4. I watch TV in the evening.
5. I go to bed at 11 o'clock.

*Part B: câu trả lời tùy bạn. Kiểm tra cấu trúc `I + verb + at + time.`*

## 01 · Sentence Building · A2
**Part A**
1. I **always drink** coffee in the morning.
2. I **am never** late for work. *(trạng từ đứng SAU "be")*
3. I **often cook** dinner at home.
4. I **sometimes go** to the gym after work.

**Part B**
5. He **watches** TV in the evening.
6. My mother usually **has** lunch at noon.
7. She **does** the dishes after dinner.

*Part C: câu trả lời tùy bạn. Ví dụ: I drink coffee before I go to work. / I go to the gym after I finish work.*
